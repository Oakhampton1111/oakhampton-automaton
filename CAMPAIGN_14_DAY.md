# Agent Desk — 14-Day Acquisition Campaign

## Objective
Generate the first 3 profitable paid engagements while measuring source-to-payment conversion and preserving positive contribution margin.

## Daily operating targets
- 10 tailored marketplace applications
- 20 targeted outbound messages
- 5 helpful community contributions
- 30 opportunities reviewed
- Follow up qualified replies within 2 hours
- No paid-ad activation until purchase attribution is verified

## Priority order
1. OpenClaw VPS rescue
2. Workflow repair
3. Automation diagnosis
4. Spreadsheet/document automation
5. API integration
6. Custom business agent

## Qualification gate
Proceed only when the post identifies a real system, operational pain, desired result, plausible authority and budget. Generic outreach is prohibited.

## Specific outreach structure
1. Reflect the exact problem and system.
2. State one relevant diagnosis or likely failure point.
3. Explain the smallest useful deliverable.
4. Give price, timing and acceptance evidence.
5. Link only to the matching service page.

## Measurement
Track service_view, service_cta, checkout_created, verified payment, gross revenue, estimated delivery cost, refund/chargeback and customer source. Pause any channel after 40 qualified clicks or 20 tailored applications with no meaningful reply unless evidence justifies another test.

## Safety
Do not fabricate case studies, identities, credentials, availability or outcomes. Do not mass-post communities. Never submit a binding bid above the configured authority cap without escalation.
